Grif privacy policy

Date: October 24, 2026

The Grif app (ru.fozar.chords) is developed by Aleksandr Timoshenko (the developer). This policy explains what data the app processes, why, who receives it and how you control your choices.

Your songs

Audio files you open in the app are analyzed on your device. The developer never receives them: the files are not sent anywhere. A copy of the file and the chords found are kept in the app's private storage so the song can be opened again from "Recent". They are removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Microphone

The microphone is used only by the tuner, to measure the pitch of a string in real time. Sound is never recorded, stored or sent. Everything except the tuner works without this permission.

Ads

The app is free and paid for by ads: a short video before a new song opens and a banner on the home screen. Ads are served by the Yandex Advertising Network (Yandex LLC); the VK Ad Network (VK LLC) takes part in the same auctions through Yandex mediation. To select and show ads and count impressions, their SDKs receive technical device data: the advertising ID, device model, operating system version, language, IP address, network connection type and ad events.

On first launch the app asks whether ads may be based on your interests. If you decline, the advertising ID is not used for personalization and ads are shown without it. You can change your choice at any time under "Ad settings" at the bottom of the home screen. You can also reset or disable the advertising ID in your device settings.

The ad networks process this data under their own policies: Yandex, VK.

Nothing else

The app needs no account and does not collect your name, phone number, e-mail, contacts or location. The developer does not sell data and does not share it with third parties other than the ad networks above. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

The app is not directed at children under 12.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent to personalized ads at any time ("Ad settings" in the app), delete all app data (clear the app's data or uninstall it), and ask the developer for information about the processing of your data or for its deletion. Data received by the ad networks can be requested or deleted through them, using the links above.

Contact

Questions about data: ifozar@yandex.ru.
